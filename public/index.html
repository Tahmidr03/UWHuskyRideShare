Husky Ride Share

Connecting UW Tacoma students for sustainable commuting

About Husky Ride Share

Husky Ride Share addresses the challenges of commuting to UW Tacoma, including high parking costs, limited parking availability, and environmental concerns. Our platform connects students who need rides (riders) with students who have vehicles and are willing to share rides (drivers).

The system enables efficient matching of ride offers and requests based on zones, times, and capacity, promoting carpooling and reducing the environmental impact of individual commuting. Users can rate their experiences, helping build a trusted community of drivers and riders.
